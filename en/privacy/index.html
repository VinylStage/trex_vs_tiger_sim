Summary

  • There are no accounts, logins or forms, and we never ask for your name, email or other personal data.
  • The site sets no cookies of its own.
  • The only thing it stores in your browser is your language choice (localStorage).
  • Every simulation runs on your device; settings and results are never sent to a server.
  • Our host, Cloudflare, processes standard request logs while delivering and protecting the site, and may set cookies that are strictly necessary for bot protection.
  • We currently use no analytics, no ads and no third-party scripts.

Who we are and scope

This policy covers the T-Rex vs Tiger Simulator at trex-vs-tiger.vinylims.com, and the same deployment served at trex-vs-tiger-sim.pages.dev and its preview addresses. The site is run by the maintainers of the open-source project VinylStage/trex_vs_tiger_sim on GitHub (“we”).

Information we collect

We do not collect personal data about visitors. The site has no accounts, comments, contact forms or newsletters, and no server-side program that receives what you type or compute. We do not identify visitors or compile their browsing for analysis.

What is stored in your browser

What the site stores in your browser
Name Type Content and purpose Retention
trexsim.lang localStorage Your interface language (ko or en), so the next visit opens in the same language. It is saved when you switch language in the simulator, or when you follow a link to the simulator from one of these help pages. Until you delete it
  • Values in localStorage stay inside your browser and are never sent to a server. They cannot be used to identify you.
  • The site itself sets no cookies. Cookies that Cloudflare may set are described under Hosting and Cloudflare.
  • If we start storing display preferences such as a colour theme, we will add them to this table before the change goes live.

Computation and share links

  • Every simulation runs in your browser's Web Workers on your own CPU. Results live in browser memory only and disappear when you close the tab.
  • Your current settings are written to the part of the URL after #s= (the fragment). Browsers do not include the fragment in HTTP requests, so these settings never reach our host or Cloudflare.
  • If you send a link made with Copy link to someone, they can see those settings (head counts, parameter ranges, seed and so on). Links contain no personal data, and whether to share them is up to you.

Hosting and Cloudflare

The site's files are served by Cloudflare Pages, a service of Cloudflare, Inc. As with any website, when your browser requests a page it sends standard information such as your IP address, the requested address (without the fragment), your browser type (user agent) and the time. Cloudflare processes this information to deliver the site and to protect it against DDoS attacks and malicious bots, and may keep standard request logs. We do not use this information to identify or track visitors.

Because of the bot protection that guards the site, Cloudflare may set these strictly necessary cookies:

Cookies Cloudflare may set
Cookie Purpose Retention
__cf_bm Bot-management cookie that tells automated traffic apart from people. It holds encrypted information used for Cloudflare's bot score and does not track you from site to site. Expires after 30 minutes of inactivity
cf_clearance Stores proof that your browser passed a check (challenge), so it is not asked again. A short period set by Cloudflare security settings

Cloudflare classifies these cookies as strictly necessary to provide its service. For details see Cloudflare cookies and the Cloudflare privacy policy. Under Cloudflare's policy, this information may be processed in data centres outside your country, including the United States.

Analytics and advertising

The site currently uses no visitor analytics, advertising, social media widgets, web fonts from other servers or any other third-party scripts. Its Content Security Policy blocks scripts from other origins.

If we introduce advertising (for example Google AdSense) or analytics to support the site, we will update this policy before they go live, name the providers and the cookies and data they use, and ask for consent where the law requires it.

External links

The site links to external sites such as the GitHub repository and research papers (DOI links). Their own privacy policies apply there. When you follow such a link, the site is configured to send only its origin (domain), never the full path or your shared settings.

Children's privacy

The site collects no personal data from anyone, whatever their age, so all visitors, including children, can use it without providing personal information.

Your choices and rights

  • You can delete this site's data (localStorage) and cookies at any time in your browser settings. The only effect is that the language returns to the default (Korean).
  • If you block localStorage the site still works; it just does not remember your language.
  • Because we hold no personal data about visitors, there is nothing for us to give access to, correct or delete. Rights concerning data processed by Cloudflare can be exercised under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update this page and the effective date at the top. Earlier versions can always be found in the change history of the GitHub repository.

Contact

For questions about privacy, open a GitHub issue. Issues are public, so please do not include personal information. Security problems can be reported privately through the repository's Security tab. More options are on the About & contact page.

T. rex and tigers never lived at the same time, and every interaction rule on this site is an assumption. The results show how an educational model responds to its assumptions; they do not predict a real fight.
